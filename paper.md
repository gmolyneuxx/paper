# Introduction 
The notion of time travel is impossible.

I hate when movies introduce time travel (Bloggs et al 2004).

Jones et al (1998) conducted a movie marathon where he just watched loads of time travel movies, concluded they made no sense.

# Motivation 

I watched Avengers Endgame and hated their choice of time travel.
